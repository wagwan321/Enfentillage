Delivery

  • Everywhere in LebanonFrom the city to the mountain villages.
  • 3 $ deliveryOne price, anywhere in Lebanon.
  • 2–6 business daysSometimes the same day, when your order is ready and the route allows.

Delivery time depends on your area, the size of the package and the season. Very large or heavy items may need special handling and a different delivery fee: we always tell you before we confirm the order.

After you order, we call to confirm the details, and we always contact you before the courier arrives. Want it gift-wrapped? Tick Wrap my gifts at checkout or just ask us.

Payment

  • Cash on deliveryPay the courier when your toys arrive.

Exchanges

You can bring a toy back within 30 days of purchase and exchange it, as long as it is:

  • unused and in its original condition,
  • in its original packaging,
  • with your proof of purchase.

Please note: we don’t offer refunds, in store or online. Exchanges are done at our store, or call us on +961 3 720 233 to arrange one.

Damaged or not working?

Bring the item back to the store as soon as possible. We’ll check it and, if it’s a manufacturing fault, contact the supplier to get you a replacement (subject to the supplier’s approval and availability).

FAQ

Where do you deliver?

All over Lebanon, north to south.

How much is delivery?

3 $ anywhere in Lebanon. Bulky items may have a special fee, which we tell you about before confirming.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 2 to 6 business days, depending on your area, the order and the time of year. Sometimes faster!

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery, or Whish Money.

Can I return a toy?

You can exchange it within 30 days if it’s unused, in its original packaging and you have the proof of purchase. We don’t offer refunds.

Do you gift-wrap?

Yes! Tick “Wrap my gifts” at checkout or tell us when you order: perfect for birthdays and baby showers.

Can I visit the shop?

Of course: we’re in Mazraat Yachouh, Chattat Center. Call +961 4 921 863 if you need directions.

About Enfantillage

Enfantillage has been a family toy store since 1992, founded by Elie and Georges Diab, a father and son who believe every child deserves to smile.

From a toddler’s first teddy to building sets and backyard adventures, we pick toys that are fun, safe and good for growing minds, from brands like LEGO, Chicco, Mattel and KAPLA. Visit us in Mazraat Yachouh, or shop online and we’ll bring the fun to your door.
